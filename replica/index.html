THE AI SHIFT & YOUR BUSINESS

Search answers now. Being the
answer is the new first place.

People used to search, scan a list of links and click. Now they ask, read one answer and act on it. Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity write that answer using sources they trust. When your website is not one of those sources, you are left out of the decision, even while your rankings look fine.

  • Ranking is no longer the finish line

    A page can sit on the first results page and still be missing from the answer written above it. Two different competitors, on the same screen.

  • Being quoted is the new click

    When an assistant names you as the source, you reach someone who has already made up their mind and is looking for who to trust with it.

  • The position is still open

    Most businesses have not adapted their websites for this yet. The ones that move now hold the answer slot while everyone else catches up.

  • Visibility only pays when the page converts

    Getting named brings the right person to your site. What happens next is conversion work: one clear offer, a short path to it, and a layout we keep testing so the decision stays easy.
